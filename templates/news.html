Version 6.1.0 - Whisper Sounds to PlayersOctober, 2026

GMs can now send soundpad sounds straight to one or more players, like a whisper sound: only the chosen players hear it, everyone else at the table hears nothing.

  • Whisper sound to player(s): Click the Send to players button () next to a soundpad sound, pick one or more connected players, and send it. Perfect for a voice only one character hears, a private vision, or a noise behind a single player's door.
  • Play once or loop: Choose to play the sound a single time or keep it looping until you stop it.
  • Stop anytime: While the sound is playing for players, the button turns green with a stop icon (). Click it again to stop the sound for them. Its tooltip shows who is hearing it.
  • Respects your settings: The sound keeps its configured volume, fade-in and fade-out, and each player still controls it with their own audio channel volume.

Players need to have clicked anywhere in Foundry at least once, because browsers block audio until the first interaction.

Version 6.0.0 - Sound Library & Sequential Loop GroupsJune, 2026

This is a big one. Soundscape Adventure now has a Sound Library: a single, global source of truth for all your sounds that makes managing, organizing, and finding sounds far easier. It also introduces a new sequential playback mode for loop groups.

  • Sound Library: A global, cross-world library where all your sounds live. It is the single source of truth, so you administer your sounds in one place instead of repeating the work for every soundscape.
  • Better organization: Browse your sounds by folder, tag them freely (folders also tag them automatically), and mark favorites.
  • Easier to find sounds: Search, filter by tags, and create synonyms so that one term surfaces all related sounds. The library also helps you spot duplicates.
  • Multiple views: You can see the sounds as a list or in the folder strucutre.
  • Minimalist ambiences: Use the library on its own to send sounds straight into Foundry playlists and build quick ambiences.
  • Sequential Loop Groups: Loop groups can now play their sounds one after another in order, looping back to the first when the last finishes.

To open the Sound Library, click the Sound Library button () in Foundry's Audio controls on the left scene toolbar.

Your existing soundscapes are migrated to the new format automatically the first time you open them, with an optional backup.

If you're experiencing any issues with the module, please let me know by creating an issue on GitHub here.
