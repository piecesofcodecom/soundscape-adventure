Version 0.5.0 - New Year New Layout Jan, 2026

The soundscape Adventure V5 has a new layout and improved user interface.

Now you can select 2 different views: Card View and Mixer View (you need to go to Game Settings -> Soundscape Adventure).

The sad news is the previous soundscape that loads sounds from internet is deprecated. Good news is new modules are coming soon this year to enhance the experience.
